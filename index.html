GOOD TO KNOW

Questions students ask us.

Is the first counselling session really free?

Yes. Your first session is free, with no obligation. You can discuss your goals and understand possible next steps.

When should I start planning?

Ideally 12 to 18 months before your intended intake. If your timeline is shorter, speak with us and we'll help you understand what may still be possible.

Can you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities and immigration authorities make those decisions. We help you prepare and submit a strong, complete application.

Do I have to use all your services?

No. You can ask for help with one area, such as test preparation, applications or a visa document review.

Can my parents join the counselling session?

Absolutely. Parents and family members are welcome to join discussions about course choices, funding and planning.
