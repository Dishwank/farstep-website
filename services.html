HOW WE CAN HELP

Help for every part of
studying abroad.

Use one service or all four. Either way, you get one counsellor who understands your plans and helps keep things moving.

01 · UNIVERSITY APPLICATIONS

Build applications with confidence.

We help you choose where to apply and prepare each application. Your counsellor reviews drafts and helps you keep track of deadlines.

What's included
  • Course and university shortlist based on your grades, budget and goals
  • Statement of purpose and CV reviews
  • Advice on recommendation letters
  • Application deadline tracking
  • Help comparing offers
02 · TEST PREPARATION

Prepare with a plan.

Build a study plan around your target score and test date, with practice and feedback.

Tests covered
  • IELTS, TOEFL, PTE and Duolingo English Test
  • GRE, GMAT and SAT
  • Full-length mock tests with feedback
  • Speaking and writing practice
  • Week-by-week preparation plan
03 · STUDENT VISA GUIDANCE

Get your documents in order.

Visa requirements vary by destination and can change. We help you organise your file and prepare for the process.

What's included
  • Document checklist for your country and course
  • Review of financial documents
  • Mock visa interview preparation where applicable
  • Appointment and biometrics guidance
  • Pre-departure briefing
04 · EDUCATION LOANS

Understand your funding options.

We explain options, help compare lender offers and prepare the documents lenders may request.

What's included
  • Secured and unsecured loan options explained
  • Side-by-side lender offer comparison
  • Loan document preparation support
  • Tuition, rent and living-cost budget
  • Guidance on sanction letters for visa files
