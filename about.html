ABOUT FARSTEP

Good advice should be
easy to find.

Farstep is a study abroad consultancy focused on helping students make informed decisions with clear guidance and practical support.

Why we started

Studying abroad is one of the biggest choices a student makes. Information can feel scattered across forums, friends, institutions and advisers. Farstep aims to make the process easier to understand with honest conversations, clear steps and useful university research.

How we work

01

One counsellor, start to finish

We aim to provide continuity so students have a familiar point of contact throughout their planning.

02

Straight answers

We discuss options, constraints and uncertainties so students can make informed choices.

03

Tools that save time

Research guides and checklists help organise the work around important decisions.

Two parts, one company
